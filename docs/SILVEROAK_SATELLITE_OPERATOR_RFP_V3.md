## Standard due-diligence request (send only after approved)
1. Can you serve (a) San Pedro de Macorís / Los Llanos, Dominican Republic and (b) Jacmel / Morne-à-Brûlé, Haiti at the intended site coordinates?
2. Is the product enterprise wholesale, managed service, or retail? **Is third-party redistribution / paid community access explicitly permitted in writing?**
3. Can Silveroak contract directly? If not, identify an authorized local integrator and the permitted reseller route.
4. What are terminal, mounting, shipping, customs, installation, activation and recurring costs? Quote USD and applicable taxes separately.
5. What are committed information rate (CIR), peak information rate (PIR), fair-use rules, caps, QoS priority, upload and download ranges?
6. What are expected RTT latency, uptime SLA, credit remedies, maintenance windows, support response and restoration times?
7. How many users can be served simultaneously at the selected committed capacity? How is congestion handled?
8. What power consumption, grounding, lightning, heat, weather, line-of-sight and terminal mounting requirements apply?
9. What public/private IP, CGNAT, IPv6, VPN, data routing and security options are available?
10. Is a redundant path/provider possible? What diversity truly exists (satellite, gateway, last mile)?
11. What regulatory authorizations, equipment type approvals, frequency use and import permits must the buyer obtain locally?
12. What are minimum term, deposit, currency risk, cancellation costs, termination rights and data processing terms?
13. Is service available for disaster response and institutions (schools, hospitals, farms), and are special rates available?
14. Please provide a written bill of materials, deployment schedule, commercial offer validity period and named technical contact.
15. May we test one site with **no public resale** until regulator and operator permissions are confirmed?

## Scoring method
Legal/redistribution right (mandatory PASS); verified geographical feasibility (mandatory PASS); total cost of ownership 25%; CIR and quality 20%; reliability/backup 20%; support 15%; integration/security 10%; community scalability 10%.
Do not rank candidates as eligible solely from generic worldwide coverage maps.
