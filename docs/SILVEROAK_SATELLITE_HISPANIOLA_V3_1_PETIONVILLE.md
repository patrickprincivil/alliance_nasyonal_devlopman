# SILVEROAK GLOBAL SATELLITE NETWORK — HISPANIOLA V3.1
## Pétion-Ville included throughout Haiti coverage
**Status:** conceptual planning, no operational hub, contracted operator, licensed resale, deployment or validated budget.

## Coverage scope
**Haiti proposed regional network centers:** Jacmel / Morne-à-Brûlé, **Pétion-Ville**, Port-au-Prince metropolitan area, Cap-Haïtien, Les Cayes, Gonaïves, Jérémie, Hinche, Miragoâne, Fort-Liberté; plus underserved rural communities. **Pétion-Ville** is explicitly a proposed regional node and must appear in coverage schedules, pilot criteria, finance, network and rollout maps. Pétion-Ville and Port-au-Prince are distinct planned sites (not an assertion of access or present service).

**Dominican Republic proposed centers:** San Pedro de Macorís / Los Llanos, Santo Domingo, Santiago, Punta Cana, La Romana, Puerto Plata, Barahona, San Cristóbal, San Francisco de Macorís, Monte Cristi.

## Network architecture
Satellite provider(s) → Haiti national coordination + Dominican coordination → regional nodes (including **Pétion-Ville**) → licensed community distribution and private installations → homes, schools, hospitals, farms, offices, cameras, offline learning and devices. Geographically separated centers generally require their own backhaul or licensed terrestrial links. A satellite footprint is not automatic last-mile Wi-Fi.

## Pétion-Ville workstream — same shared network, no duplicate platform
- Coverage study: site survey, line-of-sight, neighborhood density, fixed-line options, customer needs, bandwidth forecast.
- Resilience & safety: reliable power, secure installation, outage recovery, lawful site access; do not assume physical access is safe.
- Engineering: independent router/terminal sizing, point-to-point feasibility, managed Wi-Fi, segmentation and monitoring.
- Stakeholders: schools, healthcare sites, small businesses and local institutions; none are confirmed partners.
- Financial model: itemized CAPEX/OPEX and accessibility funding, with approved redistribution terms and taxes.
- Governance: CONATEL requirements and spectrum, customer data privacy, installation permits and contracts.
- Pilot gate: approval follows feasibility, provider terms, site safety and regulatory review; no deployment is claimed.
