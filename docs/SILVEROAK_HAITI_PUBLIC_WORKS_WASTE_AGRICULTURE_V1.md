# SILVEROAK — HAITI NATIONAL PUBLIC WORKS, CIRCULAR ECONOMY & AGRICULTURE V1
Date: 2026-10-17
Status: proposed civic/private initiative. No authority, permit, contract, funding, roadwork or recycling/farm operation claimed.

## Mandate
Coordinate feasible solutions for deteriorated roads and collapsed bridges, drainage, waste collection, sorting/recycling, compost and farming throughout Haiti. Support legitimate public agencies and communities; do not imply substitution for state authority.

## Priority corridors / pilots for feasibility review
- Cap-Haïtien ↔ Plaisance: survey route condition, bridges, flooding, drainage, safe access. Exact segments and current conditions remain unverified.
- Border approaches: survey actual border crossing access roads and choose a pilot segment only after land/permit/security assessment; do not assume a single national route.
- Municipal solid waste: site-level sanitation audits by participating municipalities, markets and transport corridors.
- Jacmel/Morne-à-Brûlé: pilot for farm, compost and solar-powered services once site tenure, water, sanitation and agricultural suitability are established.
- Other departments: phased replication contingent on evidence, staff, contracts and funding.

## Workstreams
1. ROAD-001 inventory roads and bridges: GPS point, photo, road authority, hazard type, bypass, ownership, traffic, estimated cost, engineer review.
2. BRIDGE-001 isolate hazards and seek certified structural assessment; never reopen or rehabilitate without authorized engineering and permits.
3. DRAIN-001 culverts, canal cleaning and erosion control subject to environmental review.
4. WASTE-001 routes, household and market bins, collection operator partnerships; protect workers.
5. RECYCLE-001 sorting (plastic, glass, metal, paper), buyer/offtake verification, safe handling.
6. ORGANIC-001 separate suitable organics and pilot controlled compost; exclude medical, hazardous, mixed waste and contaminated feedstocks.
7. FARM-001 soil/water tests, seed supply, farming model, compost QC, agri training and market buyers.
8. COMMUNITY-001 worker training, open procurement, complaints and public dashboards.
9. FUND-001 grants, donor governance, verified quotes, monthly unit economics, operating reserves.

## Safety, permits and legal roles
- MTPTC and relevant road authorities own public-road authorization and technical standards; private teams cannot unilaterally close, rebuild or alter highways.
- SNGRS and local government have statutory roles in solid waste governance (2017 law). Verify site-level operating permits, waste streams and enforcement obligations.
- Coordinate with municipalities, environmental and land agencies and occupational health bodies.
- Bridge inspections require qualified engineers and relevant approvals; do not have volunteers work on unstable structures.
- Do not burn mixed waste; segregate batteries, chemicals, hospital waste, sharps and e-waste with qualified waste handlers.
- Never claim a minister, mayor or agency has delegated authority without documented agreement.

## First 90 days (planning gates only)
Days 1-15: map candidate sites, stakeholder directory, risk register and baseline; verify local access/security.
Days 16-30: request written meeting/permissions and scopes from MTPTC, municipalities and waste regulator; shortlist 1 safe waste pilot and 1 road-assessment corridor.
Days 31-60: engineering surveys, material sampling, safe organic-waste pilot design, budget, bid specs and funding requests.
Days 61-90: start only permitted low-risk pilots with trained personnel, insurance, monitoring and signed oversight agreements.

## No-go gates
No road intervention without authority and engineering signoff. No dump or recycling facility without environmental compliance. No land works without tenure proof. No promises of national delivery until funding, permits, equipment and trained operators are secured.
