## Strategy
Build first-party owned assets (Silveroak website, mailing list with consent, original intellectual property) while distributing across YouTube, podcasts and social media. Third-party platform follower counts are not owned contact lists. Respect platforms' terms and customer privacy.

## First revenue channel
**Remote PC diagnostics and practical technology education**. The channel combines existing practical PC repair experience with low-cost production. Three-language content (Haitian Creole, French, English) can be recorded once and adapted into subtitles or separate voiceovers. Avoid promising guaranteed earnings or outcomes.

## Customer journey
Free helpful video → clear service/guide landing page → consent-based inquiry → diagnostic scope and quote → human approval → provider payment → service delivery → testimonial request with permission → optional maintenance.

## Product ladder — PROPOSED starting prices, CAD, not confirmed offers
1. Free: troubleshooting videos and checklists.
2. CAD 15–29: original PDF troubleshooting guides or workshop replay.
3. CAD 49–99: scheduled remote diagnostic consultation, subject to legal and technical feasibility.
4. CAD 149–299: fixed-scope training workshop for small groups.
5. Monthly subscription only after pilot demonstrates repeat value and secure fulfillment.

## 30/60/90-day delivery
Days 1–30: validate audience; publish 6 original practical videos; one basic contact page; collect opt-in leads, not sensitive data. Pilot one service offer and record real delivery costs.
Days 31–60: iterate two high-performing topics; release one original paid guide and measure inquiries/conversion, refund requests and support time.
Days 61–90: package a small workshop and assess whether recurring support fits; expand channels only if unit economics and delivery quality hold.

## Monthly scorecard
Content released, watch-to-site traffic, qualified inquiries, booked consultations, paid customers, gross receipts, payment fees, labor/time cost, refunds, gross margin, repeat sales, consent/unsubscribe rates. Zero until data collected; no fabricated dashboards.

- AI Media Engine: editing, transcript drafts, localization, caption QA; human review before publication.
- Marketing Engine: campaign scheduling and metrics via approved provider connections.

- Shared core: org permissions, approvals, audit, backups, rights registry.

## Minimum equipment
Existing phone/computer, quiet room, wired earbuds or existing mic, free editing software, simple lighting. Purchase equipment only after verifying audio quality and initial demand. Back up source recordings and release rights.

## Approval and safety
Before public publishing: human fact check, platform music licensing, usage permissions, privacy consent and editorial sign-off. Before sales: service scope, invoice/refund terms, tax/jurisdiction assessment and payment processor onboarding. Don't upload third-party copyrighted media without rights.

## Risk controls
Avoid claims about diagnosing hardware remotely without inspection; limit remote access to explicit customer permission; never request passwords or install unknown remote tools. Transparent AI usage, original content and platform-compliant marketing.
