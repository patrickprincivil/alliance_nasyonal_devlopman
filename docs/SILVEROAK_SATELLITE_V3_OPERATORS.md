## Weighted operator scorecard (score only with evidence)
- Confirmed legal operation and right to redistribute: pass/fail
- Country-wide delivery/actual site eligibility: pass/fail
- Capex / rental CPE: 15%
- Recurring unit cost including taxes: 20%
- Guaranteed committed Mbps/upstream and congestion policy: 20%
- SLA / repair / local support: 15%
- Integration to independent last-mile networks: 10%
- Resilience, latency and weather impact: 10%
- Service maturity and procurement lead time: 10%

Do not compute rankings until providers submit comparable offers. No operator is currently endorsed or partnered.

## Country regulatory gate
- **Haiti:** seek written CONATEL determination covering satellite terminal import/type approval, spectrum, service resale/public telecom operation, registration, fees and customer data rules.
- **Dominican Republic:** seek written INDOTEL determination covering satellite service, internet provision/resale and equipment homologation/other obligations.
- Consult local licensed telecom counsel and providers; a residential subscription is not a country-wide telecom operating authorization.

## Deployment modes
1. Offline LAN for schools and community resources — local content, local cameras, local GPS receiver use without internet where technically feasible.
2. Licensed connectivity pilot — satellite or fixed WAN + routed campus Wi-Fi, bandwidth caps, consent, network segmentation and measured capacity.
3. Dedicated enterprise network — separate traffic/security classes, SLA, monitoring, redundancy and budgeted support.

## Commercial procurement gates
Written quote; verified reseller permissions; regulatory clearance; tested pilot; paid OPEX budget; equipment sourcing + customs + spares; secure data policy; backup power; capacity/coverage survey; owner approval. Never publicly promise unlimited internet or free nationwide access before financing.
