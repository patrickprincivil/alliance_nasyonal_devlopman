# SILVEROAK NATIONAL CONNECTIVITY — PROVIDER RFP TEMPLATE
**Draft for vendor outreach — not sent.**
Goal: obtain independent written proposals for (A) a Dominican Republic pilot near San Pedro de Macorís, (B) a Haiti pilot near Jacmel, (C) scaled 10-node community deployment, and (D) optional business/critical-facility backup.

## Provider questionnaire
1. Confirm licensed coverage for the exact GPS coordinates of each proposed site and applicable country service policies.
2. Are you the licensed operator, authorized reseller, or distributor? Provide contracting entity and documentation.
3. Is community Wi-Fi redistribution, resale and multi-tenant public access explicitly permitted for this product? Detail user limits and conditions.
4. Quote one-off hardware, mounting, shipping, installation, customs assumptions and replacement/spares.
5. Quote recurring plan cost, minimum commitment, minimum order, activation, cancellation, deposits, taxes and currency.
6. Provide committed information rate (if any), best-effort rates, priority policy, data caps, fair-use restrictions, oversubscription and shaping rules.
7. Provide measured or modeled latency, packet loss, availability, weather resilience and performance per geography.
8. Explain terminal power, environmental limits, wind, grounding, monitoring, secure management and firmware support.
9. Clarify public IP, CGNAT, IPv6, static IP/VPN, BGP availability, VLAN/SD-WAN handoff and allowed routing.
10. Detail approved resellers/installers, local SLA, failure response and shipping/replacement timelines.
11. List required permits and radio/terminal homologation responsibilities; identify whether supplier already holds required approvals.
12. Confirm audit/privacy obligations, acceptable use, security controls and data processing location.
13. Describe 1-site, 3-site, 10-site and 100-site pricing; price protection, capacity reservation and financing.
14. Can we buy one primary plus independent backup? Clarify failover handoff and limitations.
15. Provide reference projects for education, disaster response and rural community internet, if shareable.
16. Send sample enterprise/resale agreement and support terms for legal review.

## Comparison scoring (100 points)
Legal/redistribution authorization 20; site coverage 15; total 36-month cost 20; measurable performance/SLA 15; local support 10; interoperability 10; resilience 5; deployment lead time 5.
*Disqualify proposals that do not authorize contemplated services in writing or cannot meet regulator requirements.*

## Financial bid worksheet fields
Provider, country, region, terminal model, monthly USD, hardware USD, freight USD, VAT/duty estimates, installation USD, electricity watts, target capacity Mbps, cap TB/month, activation days, contract months, redistributable Y/N, license notes, support hours, backup option, quote date, validity, source URL, sales contact, decision, approver.
