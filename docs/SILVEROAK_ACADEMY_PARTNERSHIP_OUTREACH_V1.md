# Silveroak Global Academy — University & Technical School Partnership Dossier V1

**Parent:** MASTER DEVLOPMAN → Education → Global Academy  
**Status:** internal preparation; no confirmed agreements or degree-awarding authority.

## Partnership models
1. **Referral / information:** publish links to verified institution programs without suggesting partnership or guaranteeing admission.
2. **Non-credit workshops:** Silveroak supplies training facilities or practical labs; certificates of participation only unless otherwise authorized.
3. **Approved articulation:** recognition of prior learning, transfer credits or joint offerings only through executed agreements and institutional approval.
4. **Industrial apprenticeship:** employer-supervised training with work safety, legal contracts, insurance and employment-law compliance.
5. **Research lab:** scoped pilot with IP agreement, ethics oversight, safety review and data safeguards.

## Due diligence checklist (complete before outreach)
- Official legal name, jurisdiction, registration/accreditation authority and confirmation URL.
- Authorized contact and institutional email verified independently.
- Precise program, campus/remote eligibility, teaching language and intake.
- Tuition by program and currency; date checked; citizenship/residency rules and scholarship exclusions.
- Recognition in intended employment jurisdiction; licensed-profession pathways when applicable.
- Responsibilities: instructors, facilities, insurance, examinations, credential issuer, refund policies.
- Student protection: privacy, retention, consent, accessibility, safeguarding and complaint route.
- Budget, reporting, learning outcomes, acceptance criteria and approval authority.
