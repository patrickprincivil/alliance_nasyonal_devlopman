# MASTER DEVLOPMAN — Faith & Scripture Library requirements
Status: scope documented only; no books redistributed, no religious affiliations or endorsements claimed.

## Audience and principles
Provide a free, voluntary Seventh-day Adventist-focused Bible-study section, open to people of other Christian denominations and faith communities who personally wish to study Scripture and live by God's commandments. Do not test, exclude, or discriminate against visitors based on religious affiliation. Respect conscience, freedom of belief, and privacy.

## Content modules
- King James Version (KJV) Bible: free access/search/reading and optional offline availability only using a verified lawful text source and applicable regional distribution permissions. UK Crown printing/publication rights require separate review.
- Ellen G. White: link to the official EGW Writings library and apps; offer search/navigation or small attributed quotations where permitted. Do not duplicate/rehost White Estate app or bulk contents without permission.
- Sabbath School Bible Study Guide: link to official free weekly/quarterly lessons, study schedules, discussions and teacher resources. Do not republish, adapt or translate copyrighted guide text without permission.
- Personal reading plan, Bible verse references, Sabbath study planner, optional Sabbath calendar, bookmarks, audio accessibility where legal rights permit.
- Languages targeted: Haitian Creole, French, English and Spanish; each translation must have confirmed distribution rights.
- Community access: schools, churches, families and people using local offline educational hubs; no mandatory paid subscription for Scripture study.

## Shared architecture
MASTER DEVLOPMAN → EDUCATION_MEDIA → FAITH_AND_SCRIPTURE_LIBRARY
Reuse central catalog, document rights registry, search, offline cache, optional user account and accessibility; do not create a separate parallel platform.
Fields for each resource: source, author, language, rights owner, license/permission evidence, content type, external URL, offline_allowed, republication_allowed, last_verified.
Do not expose religious-belief profile or track reading behavior without explicit consent; anonymous access by default.

## Legal and editorial gates
- The KJV is generally public domain in the USA; the UK's royal-prerogative publication rules differ.
- EGW Estate's website/apps and compilations have terms and intellectual property protections. Link rather than scrape or redistribute without approval.
- General Conference's Sabbath School lessons have copyright and republication rules. Linking to authorized online lessons is safer than copying the full lessons.
- Distinguish original Adventist sources from independent Silveroak commentary. Do not imply official Seventh-day Adventist Church partnership or endorsement.
- Keep texts accurate and contextual. Human review for religious curriculum; no coercion and no marketing profiling based on belief.
