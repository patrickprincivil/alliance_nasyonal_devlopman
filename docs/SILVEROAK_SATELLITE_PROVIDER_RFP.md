# RFP — Satellite Backhaul for Silveroak Pilot

**Not sent.** Send to approved sales channels/partners; obtain written quotes for Haiti and Dominican Republic separately.

Hello Enterprise Connectivity Team,

We are evaluating legally authorized satellite backhaul for a staged community connectivity project. The first proposed locations are San Pedro de Macorís, Dominican Republic, and Jacmel/Morne-à-Brûlé, Haiti. No network has yet been deployed.

Please provide:
1. Actual serviceability for each country and the required authorized reseller/partner.
2. Minimum initial sites, terminal hardware, installation charges and lead times.
3. Committed and burst download/upload Mbps, latency, contention, data caps, throttling and SLA/credits.
4. Monthly charges in USD, taxes, customs, shipping, support, cancellation and minimum contract.
5. Whether the offered contract expressly permits community Wi-Fi distribution, sponsored access, resale and multi-tenant commercial subscribers in each country.
6. Recommended Wi-Fi/hotspot design, number of supported concurrent users, management portal and secure monitoring API.
7. Resilience options (backup satellite/terrestrial failover), static IP/CGNAT/VPN, IPv6 and security.
8. Applicable documentation for CONATEL/INDOTEL filings, terminal homologation and local-frequency coordination.
9. Pilot quote for 1 site, scalable alternatives for 3 and 10 sites, and wholesale-volume assumptions.
10. Data-protection terms, billing data handling, support contacts and escalation paths.

Please distinguish confirmed coverage, regulatory availability and **permitted resale**. We will not treat a residential consumer subscription as authority to operate an ISP. We require an itemized quotation and compliance prerequisites before procurement.

Thank you,
Silveroak Digital Solutions — Project Development Team
