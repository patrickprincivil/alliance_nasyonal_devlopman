## Contract/RFQ decision gates
1. Explicit legal right for Silveroak's proposed multi-household commercial redistribution (not implied by residential equipment ownership).
2. Written coverage and eligibility for **Haiti** and **Dominican Republic separately**.
3. Capacity pricing: committed/peak Mbps, monthly TB, overage, oversubscription and minimum term.
4. Upfront: terminal, installation, customs, licenses, fixed-site civil works, warranty and spares.
5. Quality: latency/jitter/packet loss, SLA, support response, critical-site redundancy and outage reporting.
6. Vendor-neutral handoff to router/firewall; VLANs, public/CGNAT IP, VPN, BGP capability if needed.
7. Availability of local certified installer and lawful reseller/agent model.
8. Approved operational privacy, cybersecurity and lawful-interception obligations.

**Important:** Eutelsat's advertised community Wi-Fi and Starlink's Priority hotspot terms are product- and country-specific. Do not equate them with blanket approval to resell a commercial telecom network.
