# Satellite Network: Haiti & Dominican Republic — Regulatory Gates
Source research: 2026-10-29. Regulatory interpretation requires local qualified counsel or written regulator confirmation.

## Haiti — CONATEL
Official CONATEL licensing page distinguishes a public telecom network **concession** from an **authorization** for a separate/private or shared user network. Technical application should describe wireless frequency bands and international backhaul. Equipment homologation is not an operating license. A 2022 pilot announcement does not establish Silveroak's 2026 authority.
https://www.conatel.gouv.ht/node/115
https://conatel.gouv.ht/node/513
https://www.conatel.gouv.ht/node/511

Questions: Public hotspot vs closed school network? concession vs authorization? radio spectrum, type approval, import documents? Need for Haitian legal entity/partner? Satellite resale allowed, and whether upstream carrier's rights transfer?

## Dominican Republic — INDOTEL
Official government lists Internet access as a telecom public service potentially subject to concession. Registration in the special registry includes re-sale and satellite carrier services, depending on actual business model; INDOTEL's resale regulation requires authorization before service launch and authorized geography/contract details. Do not treat one fixed private Wi-Fi test as automatically equivalent to a public carrier operation.

## Non-negotiable
- Preconsult and get written regulator guidance for exact commercial model.
- Supplier contract explicitly permits relevant sharing/resale.
- Licensed/approved RF equipment and professional electrical installation.
- Authenticated access, network isolation, lawful retention, incident response, user disclosures.
- No public service launch, advertising claims, prepaid collection or telecom resale before all approvals.
