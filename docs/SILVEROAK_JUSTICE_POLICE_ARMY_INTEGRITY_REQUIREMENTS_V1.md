# SILVEROAK MASTER DEVLOPMAN — Justice, Police & Armed Forces Integrity Requirements V1
Date: 2026-10-17
Parent: MASTER DEVLOPMAN → GOVERNANCE_FOUNDATION → JUSTICE & SECURITY ACCOUNTABILITY
Status: civic/governance policy proposal only; not an enacted law, official investigation or live disciplinary system.

## Evidence and scope
On 23 September 2026, Haiti's CSPJ reported the examination of **159 magistrate dossiers**: 119 certified; **17 not certified**; two recorded as having abandoned posts; seven retirement cases; **14 requiring additional investigation**. These figures represent the CSPJ certification process, **not evidence that all judges are corrupt**. Source: https://cspj.ht/le-cspj-cloture-une-retraite-de-certification-des-magistrats/
A 2026 UN Panel of Experts report describes credible evidence of **collusion involving certain police officers and gangs**, including trafficking/supplying weapons or facilitating movements. It does not establish that 90% of police or military personnel are gang members. Source: https://www.haitidocs.org/doc/un-panel-experts-2026/text
Assertions that Jovenel Moïse appointed exactly 50 corrupt judges, or that 90% of police and army members are involved with gangs, must be treated as **unverified allegations**, not system data or public accusations.

## Proposed standards — justice
- Public docket of certification outcomes, dates, criteria and lawful appeals with appropriate privacy protections.
- Independent follow-up on the 14 pending investigation dossiers, with recorded dispositions and deadlines.
- Asset and conflict-of-interest disclosures as legally required; objective disciplinary standards and merit-based appointments.
- Investigations by competent independent authorities; protection for witnesses, court staff and complainants.
- Removal, prosecution or sanctions only under applicable law with individual evidence, due process, and appeal rights.

## Proposed standards — police and armed forces
- Independent vetting based on verifiable intelligence, misconduct complaints, human rights records and financial-conflict evidence; avoid collective guilt or quota-based firing.
- Anti-corruption and internal-affairs units with external civilian and judicial oversight.
- Documented suspension from sensitive duties where lawful and necessary during credible investigations.
- Forensic evidence handling, arms inventory audits, chain of custody, protected confidential reporting, anti-retaliation controls.
- Criminal referral for evidence of arms diversion, extortion, kidnapping, gang collusion and human-rights abuses.
- Reinstatement and appeal paths for individuals cleared, and protection of honest officers.
- Apply requirements separately to each institution under its legal mandate, including Haitian National Police and armed forces.

## Data / AI governance
- No public list of alleged offenders without court rulings or carefully qualified attributed official records.
- No automatic accusation, blacklist, arrest, dismissal, or criminal decision by AI.
- Sensitive submissions encrypted, role-scoped, retention-limited, independently auditable.
- Statistics need an explicit denominator, period, source, verification status and methodology.
- Civic dashboards may track published court certification statistics and institutional progress, but must not purport to be an official disciplinary authority.

## Implementation stages
1. Evidence register: source, publication date, verified fact, allegation status, correction history.
2. Institution-specific standards register and fair-process checklist.
3. Secure citizen complaint intake only after legal review, privacy risk assessment and referral arrangement.
4. Dashboard for aggregate public progress; human review of every material claim.
5. Annual independent audits and published implementation outcomes when available.

This is part of the existing Master Devlopman architecture; it must reuse shared permissions, audit and human approval, without creating a separate platform.
