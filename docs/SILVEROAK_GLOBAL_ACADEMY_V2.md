# Silveroak Global Education System V2 — Integration specification
Parent: MASTER DEVLOPMAN → Education → Global Academy.
Status: conceptual program catalog + proposed page. No external university affiliation, accepted application, tuition guarantee, training accreditation, or active course delivery.

## Reuse
Existing education catalog in lib/silveroak/education.ts is retained; Global Academy adds a complementary planning layer. Reuse central identity, roles, master registry, documents, finance, approval and audit.

## University/college/Fachhochschule record model
Institution + official URL; country; qualifications; language; online/on-campus; admission requirements; official recognition/accreditation evidence; tuition in original currency, academic intake, scholarship conditions and verified date; partner status; mapping to Silveroak project and sector; last review date.
Never treat external institution degrees as Silveroak-issued credentials.

## Arden University verification gate
User-provided preliminary estimates for MBA and MSc programs and Tier 2 eligibility must be verified against official program-specific pricing, terms, citizenship/residency criteria and intake dates. No eligibility or discount is guaranteed. Do not publish these estimates as verified live tuition.

## Manufacturing education phases
1. Safety-first fabrication pilot: welding, machine shop, electrical safety and skills assessment.
2. CAD, CNC, mechanics, heavy machinery operation and shop QA.
3. Robotics lab and telemetry/GPS systems, testing in controlled environments.
4. Authorized industrial pilot, engineering sign-off, material testing and operator certification.
5. Accredited university/college partnerships for degrees and regulated professions.
6. Expansion only when equipment insurance, funding, testing and regulations are satisfied.

## Partnership outreach workflow
- Identify partner candidates from verifiable school directories; check official recognition.
- Prepare proposal: curriculum, competencies, delivery mode, language, trainer qualifications, budgets and safeguarding.
- Identify award issuer, credential validation, academic freedom, IP rights, privacy, accessibility and data transfers.
- Human approval before sending outreach or signing agreements.
- Pilot with measurable outcomes, then negotiate a formal agreement.

## Acceptance criteria
- Display all 15 proposed departments and six-level technical pathway.
- Record university sources and last-verified dates; mark unverified figures.
- No scraped private records, false accreditation or fictitious enrollments.
- Protect learner data with consent, minimum retention and tenant access control.
- Pass CI and preview validation before production.
