## Enterprise/wholesale operator request (English)
Hello Enterprise Connectivity Team,

Silveroak Digital Solutions is evaluating a legally authorized pilot and, subject to results, regional satellite-connected community and business networks in Haiti and the Dominican Republic. Please provide separate country-specific written responses on:
1. Commercial service availability and approved local distributor/reseller route;
2. Whether and under what exact plan and contractual conditions community hotspot access, managed enterprise sharing, and paid redistribution are permitted;
3. Equipment, import/homologation and installation requirements;
4. Capacity and performance (CIR/MIR, downstream/upstream, latency, fair-use caps, contention, SLA and support);
5. Full price quotation and currency for equipment, monthly service, setup, bandwidth increases, SLA support, taxes and cancellation;
6. Service availability in San Pedro de Macorís (DR) and Jacmel (Haiti), with field-validation prerequisites;
7. Static IP/IPv6, network segregation, monitoring, failover and remote management options;
8. Activation lead time, contract duration and legal responsibilities of each partner.

This is a request for information and budgetary quotation, not a purchase commitment. Please indicate validity dates and any formal operator or regulator approvals needed.

Regards,
Silveroak Digital Solutions

## Solicitud a INDOTEL (español)
Buenos días:

Solicitamos orientación escrita sobre la clasificación regulatoria aplicable a un proyecto piloto de conectividad satelital y red Wi-Fi comunitaria/empresarial en San Pedro de Macorís, República Dominicana. Consideramos tanto servicios privados para una organización como una posible futura reventa autorizada a terceros.

Les agradeceríamos enviar los enlaces de los procedimientos vigentes, sus costos y plazos de revisión. No iniciaremos la oferta comercial hasta resolver las autorizaciones aplicables.

Atentamente,
Silveroak Digital Solutions

Cordialement,
Silveroak Digital Solutions

## Decision rule
**NO GO** if regulatory classification unresolved, resale not contractually authorized (when applicable), financing insufficient, power/fire safety unverified, privacy controls inadequate, or supplier quote incomplete. Provider proposal and national approval are independent prerequisites.
