## Exact questions for EVERY provider
1. Do you deliver legally to Haiti and/or Dominican Republic and through which contracted entity?
2. Which business / enterprise / community hotspot SKU is offered for a 20–50 concurrent-user pilot?
3. Is third-party resale or community access **expressly permitted in writing**, in each country? Under which plan/contract?
4. Can Silveroak act as a registered customer, integrator, or authorized reseller? What agreements/eligibility are needed?
5. One-time terminal, mounting, freight, taxes, customs, installation, activation and deposit costs?
6. Monthly recurring price, currency, minimum term, early termination, usage caps, overage, throttling and fair-use policy?
7. Committed and typical down/up capacity, latency, contention, coverage and uptime SLA?
8. Public IP, CGNAT, VPN, VLAN, enterprise management API, support and incident escalation?
9. Required power supply, watts idle/peak, weather tolerance, replacement lead time and warranty?
10. Terms for school/clinic nonprofit use, public hotspot, remote video and camera traffic?
11. Which licenses, registration documents or spectrum/equipment approvals are supplier responsibilities and which are ours?
12. Pilot price for 1, 3 and 10 hubs; options for a second independent failover operator?

## Starlink contractual caveat
Starlink legal terms restrict resale without authorization; some Priority terms permit community Wi-Fi/hotspots, subject to local law. The indirect reseller program has its own limitations. Do not sign a residential agreement assuming wholesale resale rights.
Official: https://www.starlink.com/legal/documents/DOC-1354-58366-77
Official: https://starlink.com/re/support/article/babe863c-25ec-9df3-c485-14471df0c88e

## RFQ message — English
Hello Sales and Partnerships Team,

Silveroak Digital Solutions is evaluating a small, compliant satellite-backed community and institutional Internet pilot in San Pedro de Macorís (Dominican Republic) and Jacmel (Haiti), with possible expansion subject to permits and funding.

Please provide a written proposal for one hub in each country, with options for three and ten hubs. We request terminal/hardware and installation costs, recurring service plans, capacity, expected speeds, contention and service-level support, commercial term and usage limits, backup connectivity options, and precise written rights for community Wi-Fi distribution or resale. Please identify which product is approved in each jurisdiction, whether a locally authorized integrator is required, and all licensing/documentation responsibilities.

No purchase or deployment has been authorized. We would appreciate your commercial contact and requirements for a preliminary technical discussion.

Silveroak Digital Solutions
