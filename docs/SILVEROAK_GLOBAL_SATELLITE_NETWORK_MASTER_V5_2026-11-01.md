## Verified official-source points reviewed 2026-11-01
1. **Starlink terms:** the current terms prohibit resale of service as a standalone, integrated or value-added service unless Starlink authorizes it. They describe a limited community Wi-Fi/hotspot exception for Priority plans, subject to the agreement and its limitations. Obtain written plan-, country- and topology-specific confirmation. Residential terms must not be treated as community resale permission.
2. **Eutelsat:** its official enterprise page markets enterprise connectivity and Community WiFi for underserved locations. This establishes a product category, not a confirmed local quote, available partner, capacity, price or regulatory right in Haiti or the Dominican Republic.
3. **Haiti / CONATEL:** published rules distinguish concessions for public networks/services (including ISP and commercial community relay services) from authorizations for independent private/shared networks and other categories. The application process asks for technical and administrative details including equipment, location, frequencies and international access. Equipment homologation alone is not a service/network operating licence.
4. **Dominican Republic / INDOTEL:** the Dominican Government service portal describes a concession for public telecom services including Internet access. Spectrum permissions and special registration may also be relevant depending on the activity. Obtain a written classification for the precise private-LAN, sponsored community access or resale design before activation.

## Operational direction — reuse the existing V8 plan
- Keep the proposed pilots DR-001 (Los Llanos / San Pedro de Macorís) and HT-001 (Jacmel / Morne-à-Brûlé) as proposals, not commissioned sites.
- Run one pilot at a time. Begin with the Dominican site only if the site host, provider contract, regulatory classification, engineering, funding and safety gates are all documented. Haiti remains a separate legal and site approval track.
- Send written provider RFIs only after the authorized signatory approves them. Ask for site-level orderability, explicit community/hotspot/resale rights, local contracting party, installer, 50/100/250 Mbps options, upload/CIR/MIR, caps, SLA, terminal/installation/customs costs, 12/36-month TCO, support and data/security terms.
- Do not buy equipment or promise service based on residential plans, marketing footprints or estimated pilot budgets.
- Maintain segmented guest, management, camera/IoT and business networks; protect exact site coordinates, credentials and camera locations. Human approval is required for procurement, firewall/routing changes, customer charges and external communications.
- National coverage remains a long-term ambition. Each regional hub requires separate backhaul, capacity, legal and power design; one terminal does not provide nationwide or cross-border Wi-Fi.
