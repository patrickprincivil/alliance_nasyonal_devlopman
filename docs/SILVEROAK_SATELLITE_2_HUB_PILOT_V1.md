# Two-Hub Satellite-Backed Network Pilot — Silveroak
Date 2026-10-29. Concepts only: no installation dates, service provider agreement, confirmed sites or license.

## Pilot A — San Pedro de Macorís, Dominican Republic
Site survey for a safe rooftop/ground mast, legal site permission, power reliability and available fiber/cellular backhaul. 1 satellite business terminal; 1 gateway/router with firewall and QoS; 1 managed PoE switch; 2 outdoor Wi-Fi access points; captive portal/access control if lawful; 1 local offline content server; monitoring, UPS and surge protection. Validate 20–50 concurrent users **only after throughput survey**. Start strictly private or test-mode until INDOTEL confirms rules for public access/resale.

## Pilot B — Jacmel / Morne-à-Brûlé, Haiti
Same modular network, adapted for off-grid solar battery and safe mounting. Priorities: offline education, local messaging, local health/farm information (not regulated medical devices), and a small number of approved Internet users. Inspect logistics, security, local partner and power storage; request CONATEL written position before public operation. Avoid assumptions that equipment import approval grants service license.

## Starter materials (quantities are planning assumptions per hub)
- 1 country-authorized business satellite terminal + matching mount/cables and service contract.
- 1 firewall-capable router, 1 managed PoE switch, 2 outdoor rated APs + secure mounting.
- 1 weather-protected network enclosure, proper earthing/lightning/surge design by qualified installer.
- 1 UPS and backup power; for Haiti, engineer-sized PV + MPPT + LiFePO4 battery after real watt-hour load test.
- 1 low-power offline educational cache / local file server, lawful digital-content licenses.
- 1 spare power supply, replacement cables, tools and weatherproof connectors.
- Monitoring software, access logs with retention control, user-consent text, labeling and backup config.
- Appropriate electrical/RF contractors, safety equipment and permits.

## Example nonbinding financial gates (USD)
- 1 pilot: initial planning envelope US$2k–5k is **not a validated price** and may be insufficient once permitted business service, freight, electrical safety and installation are included.
- 2 sites: do not simply assume double cost. Price separately per country with hardware, shipping, taxes, support, energy and site construction.
- Distinguish CAPEX from recurring bandwidth, staff, import tax, replacement reserve and contingency; require at least 3 quotes for major purchases where practical.

## Acceptance testing (4-week supervised pilot AFTER authorization)
Week 0: legal/contract/site approvals, power, safety, local stakeholders, installation plan.
Week 1: bench test; validate approved terminal, reliable power, local access and failover.
Week 2: controlled performance sampling during busy/quiet hours; speed, jitter, packet loss, 95th-percentile latency, capacity and bandwidth fairness.
Week 3: power outage and weather response, backup/restore, VPN/firewall, parental/child data safeguards, operator incident process.
Week 4: report actual costs, support tickets, user experience and go/no-go recommendation; do not scale absent demonstrated economics.

## Explicit stop conditions
No legal redistribution right; no local regulatory authorization; unsafe mount/power; no landowner permission; unmet data-protection requirements; recurrent expenses above approved budget; inability to support users or handle abuse reports.
