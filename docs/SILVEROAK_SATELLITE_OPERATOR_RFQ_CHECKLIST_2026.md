## RFI/RFQ questions
1. Are fixed enterprise sites currently serviceable in **Los Llanos/San Pedro de Macorís, Dominican Republic** and **Jacmel, Haiti**?
2. Identify legal contracting entity and authorized reseller/distributor for each country.
3. Is commercial **public community Wi-Fi sharing or ISP resale** expressly allowed? Provide precise written terms, exceptions and fees.
4. Provide monthly capacity packages, committed information rate, peak rate, contention, data caps/FUP, latency ranges, SLA and credits.
5. One-site and 10-site quotations: equipment, install, licensing, training, support, deposits, recurring service, taxes/customs, term, early exit, replacement parts.
6. Network/security: CGNAT vs public IP/IPv6, site VPN, VLANs, monitoring API, incident notice, failover, portability.
7. Physical requirements: site survey, antenna footprint, mounting, line-of-sight, lightning protection, electrical load, UPS/autonomy.
8. Installation: importation/type approval, lead times, installation partners, certified field technicians.
9. Provide sample legal agreement, data processing contract, abuse management and support escalation.
10. Explain how CONATEL and INDOTEL permits and company qualifications affect activation or permitted resale.

## Regulator inquiry — separate for both countries
- Haiti CONATEL: clarify concession for public network/ISP versus authorization for private group usage, satellite carrier relationship, radio spectrum and equipment homologation.
- Dominican Republic INDOTEL: clarify public telecom concession, Registro Especial for resale or satellite carrier activities, and applicable equipment/type approvals.
- Record written reference ID, date, officer, applicable service area, limits and expiration. Do not activate public paid access before approvals.

## Two gated pilots
### DR: Los Llanos / San Pedro de Macorís
Survey landlord/site rights, GPS point, roof and antenna obstruction, grid power, grounding and security. Pilot isolated guest Wi-Fi, 2–4 APs, switch, approved enterprise backhaul, firewall, UPS and offline content. No cameras except consensual legitimate installation.

### Haiti: Jacmel area
Repeat in a location selected through technical, financial and security review. Include offline education cache and solar feasibility; do not presume site access or safe deployment.

### Acceptance tests
- Record actual speed, jitter, latency and packet loss over peak and off-peak periods.
- Prove guest/management/IoT segmentation, strong credentials and controlled remote admin.
- Validate peak concurrent clients, data usage, bandwidth caps, uptime, backups and power failover.
- Record signed regulatory and contract approvals, operating costs, per-user economics, support tickets, maintenance and incident handling.
- Halt expansion if permissions, service reliability or financing fail.
