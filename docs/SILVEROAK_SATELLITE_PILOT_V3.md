# Silveroak satellite two-site pilot V3

STATUS: concept only. No operator agreements, permits or deployed sites.

## Pilot locations
- San Pedro de Macorís region, Dominican Republic.
- Jacmel region, Haiti.
Exact locations and availability require site surveys.

## Decision stages
1. Survey site access, electricity, grounding, view of sky, demand and terrestrial alternatives.
2. Obtain country-specific regulatory guidance from INDOTEL and CONATEL and legal written permission for the intended multi-user service model.
3. Request enterprise quotes for terminals, service, installation, warranty, SLA and local support.
4. Estimate site CAPEX and recurring OPEX with contingency, import taxes, spares and staff costs.
5. Require documented funding, risk review and human approval before procurement.
6. Where permitted, test secure router/firewall, isolated guest Wi-Fi, UPS, monitoring, backup and offline educational content.
7. Validate throughput, latency, availability, security, power recovery and service support before expansion.

## Notes
A satellite footprint is not Wi-Fi coverage. Each hub needs licensed local distribution infrastructure and capacity sized to measured users. Physical installation needs a qualified professional for rooftop work, grounding and electrical safety. Published budget ranges are placeholders, not verified operator prices. Network expansion is gated by legal authorization and funding.
